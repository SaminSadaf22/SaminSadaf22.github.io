Corn Leaf Disease Detection Using Deep Learning

Undergraduate thesis · 2026

A comparative study of DenseNet121 and EfficientNet-B0 for classifying corn leaf disease from images, developed as my thesis at Daffodil International University.

DenseNet121 · EfficientNet-B0 · Transfer learning

  • Agricultural computer vision — Disease detection from leaf imagery, where lighting, background and class imbalance are the real difficulty.
  • Evaluation under uncertainty — Per-class reporting, calibration and split hygiene — what decides whether an accuracy figure means anything.
  • Efficient transfer learning — What a pretrained backbone buys when compute and labelled data are both limited.
